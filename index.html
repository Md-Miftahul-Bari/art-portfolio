About

A painter with an art-historian’s eye.

Born and raised in Rajshahi, I am a third-year student pursuing a BFA in History of Art at the University of Rajshahi.

My practice runs from large-scale murals for cafés, restaurants and institutions to brand identities, posters and packaging. Studying art history sharpens how I read images and compose my own — I care as much about why a picture works as how it looks. Alongside the studio I am an active debater, and a traveller who looks for culture and new experiences wherever I go.
